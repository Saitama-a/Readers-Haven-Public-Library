Our Library Services

Readers' Haven Public Library offers a variety of services to make reading, learning and research easier and more accessible for everyone. Explore our facilities and discover how we can help you.

Explore Our Services

We provide convenient facilities for students, researchers and book lovers.

1. Online Book Catalogue

Search our library collection online and find books using their title, author or category. Check book details and availability before visiting.

How to Use:

  1. Open the library's online catalogue.
  2. Enter the book title or author's name.
  3. View the book details and availability.

Availability: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

2. E-Books and Digital Reading

Library members can access digital books and reading materials online without physically visiting the library.

How to Use:

  1. Sign in using your library membership.
  2. Browse the available digital collection.
  3. Select an e-book and start reading.

Availability: 24/7 digital access.

3. Study Room Booking

Students and study groups can use our quiet study rooms for exam preparation, research and collaborative learning.

How to Book:

  1. Visit the library reception desk.
  2. Ask about available study rooms.
  3. Confirm your preferred booking time.

Timings: Monday to Saturday, 9 AM – 6 PM.

4. Printing and Scanning

Visitors can print assignments, educational documents and study materials. Scanning facilities are also available for digital copies.

How to Use:

  1. Visit the printing and scanning counter.
  2. Provide your document to the staff.
  3. Select the required printing or scanning option.

Timings: Monday to Saturday, 9 AM – 5 PM.

5. Free Wi-Fi Access

Readers can use the library's free Wi-Fi to access educational websites, conduct online research and enjoy digital reading.

How to Connect:

  1. Enable Wi-Fi on your personal device.
  2. Select the Readers' Haven network.
  3. Get the access credentials from reception.

Availability: During library opening hours.

Need Assistance?

Our library staff is always ready to help you understand and access our services. Feel free to contact us for further information.
