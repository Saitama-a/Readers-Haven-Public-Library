Become a Member

Join Readers' Haven and borrow from thousands of books across our collections. Choose the plan that fits you best.

Student Plan

PKR 1,000 / year

  • Borrowing limit: 3 books at a time
  • Loan period: 14 days
  • Renewals: 1 time
  • Valid student ID required

Adult Plan

PKR 2,500 / year

  • Borrowing limit: 5 books at a time
  • Loan period: 21 days
  • Renewals: 2 times
  • Valid CNIC required

Family Plan

PKR 4,500 / year

  • Borrowing limit: 10 books at a time (shared)
  • Loan period: 21 days
  • Renewals: 2 times
  • Covers up to 4 family members

Late-Return Rules

  • A late fee is charged for every day a book is returned after its due date.
  • Borrowing is paused until all overdue books are returned and fees are paid.
  • Books overdue by more than 30 days are treated as lost and the replacement cost is charged.
  • Books can be renewed before the due date if no other member has reserved them.

How to Join

  1. Visit the front desk with your ID (student ID or CNIC).
  2. Fill out the membership form.
  3. Pay the annual fee for your chosen plan.
  4. Collect your library card and start borrowing.
